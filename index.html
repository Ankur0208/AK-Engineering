Fire Protection Systems

Select a system to see what we provide.

Fire Hydrant System

External and internal water-based firefighting networks for reliable water supply across the premises.

  • Hydrant landing valves, hose reels and pipe network
  • Fire pump sets (main, jockey, standby)
  • Underground / above-ground piping and water storage interface
  • Testing and commissioning

Fire Pump

Pump sets that keep the fire network pressurised and ready to deliver water the moment it is needed.

  • Main, jockey and standby pump sets
  • Pump controllers and control panels
  • Suction and delivery piping with valves
  • Flow testing and commissioning

Fire Sprinkler System

Automatic sprinkler protection that controls fire at its early stage in buildings and warehouses.

  • Wet, dry and pre-action layouts as required
  • Sprinkler heads, control valves and flow switches
  • Hydraulic design as per applicable codes
  • Installation, flushing and hydro-testing

Gas Suppression System

Clean-agent protection for areas where water damages equipment, such as server rooms, control rooms and electrical panels.

  • Agent cylinders, nozzles and release panels
  • Detection-linked automatic discharge
  • Room integrity and safety interlocks
  • Commissioning and handover

Fire Alarm System

Early detection and alert system that gives occupants time to respond.

  • Smoke and heat detectors, manual call points
  • Addressable control panels and hooters
  • Integration with suppression and building systems
  • Programming, testing and handover
